Delete your Captivio account

Captivio (com.captiono.app) by Viktor Koniushev (developer name on Google Play: Konyushev).

How to request deletion

  1. In the app: open Captivio → Account → Delete account, and confirm. The account is deleted immediately.
  2. On the web, without the app (accounts with e-mail and password): open the account deletion form, enter your e-mail and password and confirm. The account is deleted immediately.
  3. By e-mail: write to konyushev.app@gmail.com from the address of your account with the subject "Delete my Captivio account". We delete the account within 7 days and reply to confirm.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

What is deleted

  • Your account: e-mail, name, sign-in methods, password hash, sessions.
  • All projects: uploaded videos, finished videos, previews, masks and analysis files, transcripts, translations, edits, settings and saved style templates.
  • Usage records (minutes, translations, exports). Jobs still running are cancelled.

What may be kept, and for how long

  • Security log: one entry that an account was deleted (account number and time, no e-mail or content) — up to 90 days, to protect against abuse.
  • Database backups made before the deletion — until they are replaced, at most 14 days. Backups contain no videos.
  • Server request logs (IP address, time, request path) — until overwritten in rotating log files.
  • Google Play purchase record (purchase token, product, state) — kept without any link to your account, as a record of the purchase. Google keeps its own payment records.
