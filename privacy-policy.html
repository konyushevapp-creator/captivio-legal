Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026. Applies to the Captivio Android app (com.captiono.app) and its server.

Captivio is developed and operated by Viktor Koniushev, an individual developer in Georgia (developer name on Google Play: Konyushev) ("we", "us"). He is responsible for the processing of personal data described here. Contact for all privacy questions and requests: konyushev.app@gmail.com.

1. What Captivio does

Captivio adds captions to your videos. You upload a video from your phone; our server recognises the speech, translates the text if you ask it to, lays the captions over the video in the style you choose and gives you the finished video to download. The processing runs on Captivio's own server.

3. Who processes data for us and who receives it

  • Speech recognition and translation run with our own models (Whisper and MADLAD-400) on our own server. Your video, audio and text are not sent to any outside recognition or translation service.
  • Hosting: netcup GmbH, Germany. The server and all stored data are located in the European Union.
  • E-mail delivery: when we send you a confirmation or password-reset code, an e-mail delivery provider receives your e-mail address and the text of that message.
  • Google: if you sign in with Google (when this option is offered in the app), we receive from Google a confirmation of the sign-in, your e-mail address and name. If you buy a subscription, the payment is handled by Google Play; we receive the purchase token and check its state with Google; we never receive your card details.
  • Authorities, only where a decision binding on us requires it.

4. Security

All connections use HTTPS. Files are reachable only through signed links that are valid for 15 minutes and bound to your account. Passwords are stored only as hashes. Password and code guessing is rate-limited. Database backups are stored on the server, contain no videos and are kept for up to 14 days.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Delete a project in the app at any time — its texts, settings and files are deleted.
  • Delete your account and all its data in the app (Account → Delete account), on the web (how to delete your account) or by e-mail. Details of what is deleted and what is kept are on that page.
  • To get a copy of your data, correct it or object to processing, write to konyushev.app@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.
  • If you live in the EU, the EEA or the UK, you may complain to your data protection authority.

Legal bases (EU/UK): performing our contract with you (providing the service), our legitimate interests (security, preventing abuse, fixing errors) and legal obligations.

6. Children

Captivio is not intended for people under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data of children.

7. Changes

We announce significant changes to this policy in the app before they apply. The date at the top shows the latest version.
